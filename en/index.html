    Services

    What happens at the clinic?

    Every animal is assessed by examination; the headings are general information.

    • stethoscope

      General check-up

      Weight, temperature, mouth and ear checks; questions on diet and care.

    • vaccines

      Vaccination schedule

      Puppy, kitten and adult vaccines; the next date is written on the card.

    • bug_report

      Parasite treatment

      Internal and external parasite treatments with seasonal reminders.

    • science

      Laboratory

      Blood and stool tests; results are reviewed together at the visit.

    • memory

      Microchip and passport

      Microchipping and pet passport paperwork.

    • content_cut

      Nail and ear care

      Nail trimming and ear cleaning in a quiet room.

    Visit guide

    Before, during and after

    In an emergency go to the nearest 24/7 emergency vet; this demo clinic does not provide emergency care.

    01Before the visit

    • event

      How is an appointment confirmed?

      Your chosen time is sent as a request; the clinic calls you to confirm.

    • pets

      How should I bring my cat?

      In a closed carrier, ideally covered with a cloth.

    02During the visit

    • badge

      Should I bring the vaccine card?

      Yes; previous vaccine and test records make the check-up easier.

    • timer

      How long does a check-up take?

      Slots are 20 minutes; extra time is planned if needed.

    03After the visit

    • event_repeat

      How will I remember the next vaccine?

      The date is written on the card and a reminder is sent a few days before.

    • emergency

      What if my pet gets worse?

      In an emergency go to the nearest 24/7 emergency vet; this demo clinic does not provide emergency care.
